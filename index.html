Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about class types, booking, or policies? Find clear answers to help you get started with confidence.

What is the cancellation policy?

Classes can be cancelled up to 24 hours before the scheduled time for a full refund.

Do I need to bring my own mat?

We provide mats for all classes, but you are welcome to bring your own if you prefer.

Can I book a class online?

Yes, use the search and booking feature in the Classes section to reserve your spot.
